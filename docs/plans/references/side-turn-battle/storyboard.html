Road of Nogg · hex battle · interpretation check

New battle flow, as I understand it

A storyboard of your brief before I touch any code: moving by clicking the board, attacking by clicking the enemy, and icons on the unit, all inside a whole-team turn with no speed order. Anything marked as a question is a call I made that you should confirm or correct.

Whole-team turns

No speed order. On your turn you command every one of your units in any order. Then the enemy side takes its whole turn.

Spent units go dark

Once a unit has acted it's darkened for the rest of the turn. When every unit is dark the turn ends by itself, or you can press End turn at any time.

Move, then act

Click a cell to move and click an enemy to attack. Magic works only if the unit hasn't moved this turn.

Always a preview

Pointing at a target shows a sword over it and a box with its name and the expected damage, before anything happens.

Leaves the screen: party panel, round order panel, seven-plate command column, prompt box. The unit readout (bottom left) stays for now.

White outline = walkable · red cell = in attack reach · gold = preview · dark unit = used this turn

Calls I made that you should check

Does moving alone use the unit up?

My read: no. A unit goes dark only when it attacks, casts, uses an item or waits. A unit that has only moved stays bright, so you can come back to it, and you can undo the move until it acts.

Can a unit move after it attacks?

My read: no. Acting ends the unit, as in Fire Emblem and Advance Wars. The game allows act-then-move today, so this is a rule change.

Does one click attack, or does it need a confirm?

My read: the hover preview is the confirmation, so one click attacks. Spells are the same: aim, see the boxes, click.

Which enemies get the sword?

My read: only enemies the unit can hit from where it stands. An enemy you'd have to walk to first shows the preview box but no sword, and clicking it just inspects it.

What happens to parties?

My read: parties stay as a way to group units, but a turn belongs to a whole side. Every party on team 1 acts together, then team 2. In a four-party battle that's two turns per round, not four.

Does the Wait icon stay?

My read: yes. It darkens a unit without acting, for when you want to keep a unit still but still let the turn end by itself.

Should End turn ask first while units are still bright?

My read: yes, a small "2 units can still act, end turn?" prompt, since misclicked End turn is a known complaint in Into the Breach.

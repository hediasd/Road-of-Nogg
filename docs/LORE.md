## 1. The Magic of Emotion and Will (The Ten Elements)

In the realm of Nogg, the ten elements do not merely represent physical phenomena; they are the manifestation of feelings, emotions, and profound philosophical concepts channeled from elemental planes. 

**Moral Neutrality:**
No element is inherently "good" or "evil." They are completely morally neutral, capable of both greatness and ruin depending entirely on the wielder's intent. 

**1. Fire (Animus / Audentia)**
*   **Concept:** Courage, Valour, and Glory. "A heart that knew fear yet chose to overcome it."
*   **Greatness:** Heroic resolve, protecting the weak, and inspiring others to act.
*   **Ruin (Excess - Wrath / Lack - Cowardice):** Reckless zealotry, destructive rage, and a burning desire that consumes oneself and allies.

**2. Thunder (Justitia)**
*   **Concept:** Justice, Fairness, Drive, and Impartiality. The moderation between selfishness and selflessness.
*   **Greatness:** Righteous protection, fairness, and swift retribution against true wrongs.
*   **Ruin:** Uncompromising judgment, tyrannical rule, and the deafening strike of punishment without mercy.

**3. Darkness (Adagnitio)**
*   **Concept:** The Hidden, Mystery, and Wisdom. Within mystery lies potential and infinite possibility.
*   **Greatness:** Sanctuary, inner peace, the preservation of sacred secrets, and self-reflection. Dedication to uncovering the unknown.
*   **Ruin:** Deceit, isolation, blinding paranoia, and the celebration of raw power above all else.

**4. Ice (Disciplina)**
*   **Concept:** Duty, Discipline, Logic, and Respect.
*   **Greatness:** Restraint, structure, surviving extreme hardship, and a clear, calculating mind.
*   **Ruin (Excess - Apathy):** Emotional suppression, rigid inflexibility, and cracking under intense pressure.

**5. Wood**
*   **Concept:** Generosity and Kindness.
*   **Greatness:** Nurturing growth, providing shelter, selflessness, and mending the wounds of others.
*   **Ruin:** Smothering over-protectiveness, naive gullibility, and forcibly altering others to fit a personal vision of 'good'.

**6. Light (Clementia)**
*   **Concept:** Faith, Moral Salvation, and Order. Seeing beauty in revelation and transparency.
*   **Greatness:** Illuminating truth, providing societal order, and unwavering belief in a higher cause.
*   **Ruin (Excess - Pride / Megalomania):** Blinding arrogance, rigid dogma, and a self-righteous god-complex.

**7. Earth (Humilitas)**
*   **Concept:** Humility, Tradition, Honour, and Loyalty.
*   **Greatness:** Unwavering reliability, honoring history, bearing the heavy burdens of others, and celebrating cultural bonds.
*   **Ruin:** Stagnation, a stubborn refusal to adapt, and being forever trapped by old grudges.

**8. Water**
*   **Concept:** Temperance, Empathy, and Adaptability.
*   **Greatness:** Moderating extremes, deep emotional understanding, and the resilient ability to flow through any hardship.
*   **Ruin:** Losing one's own identity to please others (codependency), or drowning in the melancholy of others' grief.

**9. Steel (Resolutio)**
*   **Concept:** Resolve, Creativity, Innovation, Progress, and Revolution.
*   **Greatness:** The drive to forge a better future, dismantling broken systems, and unyielding loyalty to a cause.
*   **Ruin (Excess - Arrogance):** Ruthless pragmatism (the ends justify the means), mechanizing the soul, and an ambition that grinds others to dust.

**10. Wind**
*   **Concept:** Freedom and Curiosity.
*   **Greatness:** The pure joy of discovery, unshackled liberty, and the sweeping spread of new ideas.
*   **Ruin:** Flightiness, avoiding responsibility, abandoning others, and the chaotic winds of severe anxiety.

*(Note: "None" or "Neutral" represents the absence of elemental magic—perhaps profound apathy or the purely mundane—rather than an element itself.)*

**Emotional Evolution:**
An individual's elemental affinity is fluid and inextricably linked to their psyche. As a being endures trauma, experiences growth, or shifts their worldview, the element they channel can change to reflect their new emotional state (e.g., a caster of Light freezing into Ice after a tragedy).

## 2. The Path of Ascension (The Pyramid of Belief)
The towering "bosses" and high-ranking entities of Nogg are not distinct, separate species from the general populace; they are natural evolutions. A base creature (such as a lowly slime) can undergo metamorphoses known as Ascensions (e.g., Slime -> Slime Knight -> Slime King). Ascension requires three components:
1.  **Elemental Dominance:** A profound mastery over their chosen emotional element.
2.  **Relics of Power:** The possession of potent items or artifacts that act as focal points.
3.  **The Pyramid of Belief:** The most critical component. Ascension requires the faith, trust, or reverence of others. An entity ascends by becoming a focal point for the collective belief of their lesser peers. 

*The Cosmic Hierarchy:* There is no strict limit to how many times one can ascend, though each tier requires an exponentially larger base of followers. It should be noted that mortals cannot ascend into "Primal Entities"—not because Primal Entities are a higher tier of power, but because they are a fundamentally different, parallel classification of life.
*The Ecology of Faith (Scale & Distance):* Belief is a deeply localized resource. An emotional uprising or a massive shift in faith in one Patreonate is highly unlikely to affect far-off Dominions or Masters initially. The world is vast enough that localized belief systems insulate regions from each other.
*Global Cataclysms & Cycles of Change:* While distance insulates Patrons, a true global cataclysm (such as the coming of the Red Moon) that induces worldwide, singular emotion (like pure Terror) acts as a siphon. By monopolizing the world's emotional output, an entity like Terrorugon could theoretically starve the entire Pantheon of Patrons of their localized belief. Such catastrophic shifts represent the natural cycles of change in Nogg.
*The Consequence of Doubt:* If an Ascended being loses the faith of their followers (either through localized apathy, or their followers shifting their emotion to a global fear), they do not physically devolve or shrink back to their base form. Instead, they become stagnant, irrelevant husks—hollow monuments of forgotten glory, severely lacking in the power they once wielded.

**The Journey and the Bond (Entity Viability):**
Because Ascension is fueled by external belief, any creature—no matter how humble its origin—possesses the potential to challenge gods. A lowly Slime found in a beginner's Meadow can be recruited and raised by a traveling party. Through the unwavering trust, investment, and belief of their companions (the Player), that simple Slime can Ascend repeatedly, eventually gaining the immense power necessary to delve into Chaotic Abysses and defeat nightmare entities like the Golliaggves. Player attachment is literally the magical catalyst for endgame viability.

## 3. The Rules of Engagement (Combat Mechanics)

- **Turn Flow**: Skirmishes are tactical 4v4 grid battles. Combatants act in a "Round Queue" dictated by their Speed (highest acts first). Ties in speed are resolved deterministically.
- **Actions**: During a turn, an entity may move orthogonally and then choose to perform a Basic Attack (melee), cast a Spell (utilizing elements, range, and area of effect), or Wait.
- **Damage**: Physical clashes follow a deterministic baseline: `max(1, attacker.ATK + action_power - target.DEF)`. This raw force is then modified by elemental affinities, passive abilities, and statuses.

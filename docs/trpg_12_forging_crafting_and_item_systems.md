# Aspect 12: Forging, Crafting & Item Systems

A comparative guide on item forging, weapon upgrading, item world dungeon leveling, TM move crafting, and material synthesis across TRPGs.

---

## 1. Equipment Forging & Upgrade Systems

Crafting systems where players spend materials and currency at a forge or smithy to upgrade base equipment.

### Approach 1.1: Blacksmith Forging & Stat Refinement
Players spend currency and raw materials at a smithy to upgrade base weapon stats, hit rates, critical chance, or reduce weight penalties.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Fire Emblem Series, Triangle Strategy, Front Mission Series*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Fire Emblem / Triangle Strategy:* Blacksmith forging boosts weapon damage, accuracy, and reduces weight.

### Approach 1.2: Relic Upgrades & Recipe Crafting
Recipes and materials dropped by enemies are combined to upgrade weapons into elemental relics.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Tactics Ogre / Stella Deus:* Recipes and material drops are combined to upgrade weapons into elemental relics.

### Approach 1.3: In-Item Procedural Dungeon Leveling (Item World)
Every individual item contains a procedural multi-floor dungeon that can be cleared to increase item stats and transfer passive buffs.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Disgaea Series*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Disgaea:* The **Item World**. Clearing floors levels up the item; defeating "Innocents" inside gear allows moving stat buffs between weapons.

### Not Applicable / Absent
No equipment forging, relic upgrade, or in-item dungeon leveling systems exist.
* **Games:** *Final Fantasy Tactics, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Vanguard Bandits, Into the Breach, Dofus & Wakfu, Breath of Fire Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Langrisser Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Wild Arms XF, Yggdra Union, Resonance of Fate, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Energy Breaker & Feda: Emblem of Justice, Pokémon Series, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

## 2. Ability & Spell Crafting / Synthesis

Crafting mechanics where spells are engraved, TMs are crafted from wild drop materials, or items are synthesized in alchemical furnaces.

### Approach 2.1: TM Move Crafting & Item Synthesis
TM Machine crafting consumes wild Pokémon materials + League Points (LP) to craft battle move TMs; Apricorn crafting converts apricorns into specialized Pokéballs.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series (TM Crafting & Cram-o-matic)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Series:* TM Machine crafting consumes wild Pokémon materials + League Points (LP) to craft battle move TMs; Apricorn crafting converts apricorns into specialized Pokéballs.

### Approach 2.2: Spell Coin Engraving & Status Fusion
Coins (spells) are engraved to add status effects (Poison, Stun) or expand AoE size at higher RAP costs.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Hoshigami:* Coins (spells) are engraved to add status effects (Poison, Stun) or expand AoE size at higher RAP costs.

### Approach 2.3: Monster Poaching & Soul Transmutation
Tamed monsters are poached for rare gear, or fallen ally souls are transmuted into weapons.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Final Fantasy Tactics, Knights in the Nightmare*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *FFT:* Tamed monsters are sent to the Poachers Guild to unlock rare dragon-scale armor and holy swords in secret shops.
  - *Knights in the Nightmare:* Transmutes fallen ally souls into weapons to transfer combat skills and elemental affinities.

### Not Applicable / Absent
No spell coin engraving, TM move crafting, monster poaching, or soul transmutation systems exist.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Triangle Strategy, Disgaea Series, Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Vanguard Bandits, Into the Breach, Dofus & Wakfu, Breath of Fire Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Langrisser Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Front Mission Series, Wild Arms XF, Yggdra Union, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Resonance of Fate, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Energy Breaker & Feda: Emblem of Justice, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

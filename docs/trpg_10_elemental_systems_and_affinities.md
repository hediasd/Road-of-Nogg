# Aspect 10: Elemental Systems & Affinity Matrices

A comparative breakdown of elemental wheels, 18-type matrices, terrain interactions, custom spell creation, and deity affinities across TRPGs.

---

## 1. Elemental Advantage Matrices

Architectural systems categorizing elements into advantage/weakness cycles and stat multipliers.

### Approach 1.1: 18-Type Multiplicative Affinity Matrix
18-Type Elemental Matrix where elemental multipliers stack across dual-type defenders (2x, 4x, 0.5x, 0.25x, 0x immunity).
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series (Mainline & Spinoffs)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Series:* 18-Type Matrix (Normal, Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Ice, Fighting, Poison, Ground, Flying, Psychic, Bug, Rock, Ghost, Dragon, Steel, Dark, Fairy). Multipliers stack across dual-type Pokémon (2x, 4x, 0.5x, 0.25x, 0x immunity).

### Approach 1.2: Classic 4/6 Element Advantage Circle
Standard elemental circle (Fire > Wind > Earth > Water > Fire; Light $\leftrightarrow$ Dark) where matching elements deal bonus damage or receive affinity resistance.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Final Fantasy Tactics, Triangle Strategy, Dofus & Wakfu, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Wild Arms XF, Energy Breaker*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Tactics Ogre / FFT:* Standard 4/6 element circle.
  - *Hoshigami:* Deity coin alignment alters unit element, changing spell RAP costs and elemental resistances.

### Approach 1.3: Dynamic String & Custom Word-Parsing Magic
Spells are constructed dynamically by players writing custom text strings, which the game parses into elements, ranges, and power levels.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Treasure of the Rudras (Rudra no Hidou)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Treasure of the Rudras:* Kotodama system allows players to type custom 6-letter words; the game engine parses prefixes and suffixes to determine spell element (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth, Light, Dark), MP cost, and AoE shape.

### Approach 1.4: Material Advantage Triangles (Silk > Wood > Iron)
Units and attacks belong to material categories forming a rock-paper-scissors advantage triangle.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Kartia: The Word of Fate*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Kartia:* Material advantage triangle: Silk > Wood > Iron > Silk.

### Not Applicable / Absent
Combats use non-elemental stat calculations or physical weapon triangles instead of elemental damage wheels.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series (Weapon Triangle instead), Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Disgaea Series, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Vanguard Bandits, Into the Breach, Breath of Fire Series, Langrisser Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Front Mission Series, Yggdra Union, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Resonance of Fate, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Feda: Emblem of Justice, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

## 2. Environmental Tile & Weather Interactions

Rules governing how spells or field conditions alter physical grid tiles or apply global damage multipliers.

### Approach 2.1: Global Weather & Terrain Field Modifiers
Field weather conditions apply global damage multipliers, accuracy changes, or stat boosts to matching elemental types.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series (Weather & Terrain Systems)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Series:* Field Weather (Harsh Sunlight boosts Fire/weakens Water; Rain boosts Water/weakens Fire; Sandstorm boosts Rock SpDef; Snow boosts Ice Def). Terrains (Grassy, Electric, Psychic, Misty) modify move power and grant status immunities.

### Approach 2.2: Surface Element Modifications (Burning, Freezing, Electrifying)
Casting elemental spells alters physical grid tile properties, creating surface hazards or conductive tile reactions.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Triangle Strategy, Dofus & Wakfu*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Triangle Strategy:* Fire spells burn grass; Ice spells freeze water into ice; Lightning spells conduct through water pools.

### Approach 2.3: Geo Panel & Tile Affinity Buffs
Grid tiles feature color-coded Geo Panels that grant field-wide buffs or hazards (Invincible, ATK +50%, Damage 20%).
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Disgaea Series, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Wild Arms XF*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Disgaea:* Destroying Geo Symbols triggers chain-reaction tile color changes and map damage.

### Not Applicable / Absent
Grid tiles have uniform environmental properties with no element surface hazards, weather systems, or Geo Panels.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series, Final Fantasy Tactics, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Vanguard Bandits, Into the Breach, Breath of Fire Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Langrisser Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Front Mission Series, Yggdra Union, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Resonance of Fate, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Energy Breaker & Feda: Emblem of Justice, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

# Aspect 11: Shop, Economy & Merchant Systems

A comparative study of shop structures, mercenary recruitment, secret merchants, political assemblies, and player economies across TRPGs.

---

## 1. Commercial Outfitting & Merchant Systems

Architectural shop setups where players spend gold/currency on gear, items, consumables, and secret promotions.

### Approach 1.1: Town Stores, PokéMarts & Secret Merchants
World map or town hub shops where players purchase weapons, armor, accessories, items, Pokéballs, and secret promotion items.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series (PokéMarts & Department Stores), Fire Emblem Series, Shining Force Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Breath of Fire Series, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Feda: Emblem of Justice, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Series:* PokéMarts, Department Stores, Battle Frontier BP shops, and Game Corner vendors selling Pokéballs, Potions, TMs, and Held Items.
  - *Fire Emblem / Shining Force:* World map shops + secret merchants accessible on specific grid tiles using secret keys.

### Approach 1.2: Base Camp Outfitters & Mobile Supply Wagons
Player accesses shops directly from a mobile hub base camp or battle preparation screen before deploying units.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Berwick Saga, Triangle Strategy, Fire Emblem (Three Houses / Engage)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Berwick Saga / Triangle Strategy:* Base camp outfitter and mobile supply wagon accessible during battle prep.

### Approach 1.3: Wanzer Mech Part Assembly Dealers
Shops sell individual Wanzer robot components (Body, Left Arm, Right Arm, Legs, Computer Processors, Shoulder Backpacks, Missiles).
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Front Mission Series (FM 1–5)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Front Mission:* Mech assembly shops selling individual limb parts, processors, and weapons.

### Not Applicable / Absent
No commercial shops or merchant outfitting systems exist; gear is acquired strictly through battle drops or card setups.
* **Games:** *Final Fantasy Tactics, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Disgaea Series, TearRing Saga, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Vanguard Bandits, Into the Breach, Dofus & Wakfu, Treasure of the Rudras, Langrisser Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Wild Arms XF, Yggdra Union, Resonance of Fate, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Energy Breaker*

## 2. Unit Recruitment & Political Economies

Economy systems for hiring custom units, maintenance troop costs, or voting to modify shop inventories.

### Approach 2.1: Mercenary Guilds & Custom Unit Creation
Shops or offices where players spend gold/currency to recruit custom generic mercenaries or generate custom recruits.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Final Fantasy Tactics, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *FFT / Tactics Ogre:* Guilds / Soldier's Offices where players pay gold to generate custom recruits.

### Approach 2.2: Commander Mercenary Troop Maintenance Costs
Before each battle, commanders spend funds to hire mercenary squad troops (e.g. Spearmen, Archers, Cavalry).
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Langrisser Series, Master of Monsters / Nectaris*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Langrisser:* Commanders spend funds before battle to hire mercenary troop stacks.

### Approach 2.3: Dark Assembly Senate Voting & Player Markets
Mechanics allowing players to influence shop inventories through political votes or trade directly with other players.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Disgaea Series, Dofus & Wakfu*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Disgaea:* Dark Assembly senate where players spend Mana to pass bills (e.g. "Better Stock in Shops!").
  - *Dofus & Wakfu:* Player-driven auction houses and markets where players trade crafted gear and resources.

### Not Applicable / Absent
No mercenary recruitment guilds, troop maintenance costs, or political shop assemblies exist.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series, Triangle Strategy, Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Vanguard Bandits, Into the Breach, Breath of Fire Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Vandal Hearts Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Front Mission Series, Wild Arms XF, Yggdra Union, Resonance of Fate, Energy Breaker & Feda: Emblem of Justice, Pokémon Series, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

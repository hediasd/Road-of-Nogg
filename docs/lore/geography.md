# Geography and Regions of Nogg

The world of Nogg is expansive, diverse, and politically complex, heavily shaped by the distribution of elemental energy and the Ascended beings who claim dominion over its lands.

## Political Structures

The lands of Nogg are categorized into several distinct political and territorial structures, often defined by the power or influence of their rulers:

*   **Governorates:** Major political regions often centered around a vast, highly populated city or a significant economic hub. Examples include the *Governorate of Crocodopolis* and the *Governorate of Starhallsen*.
*   **Patreonates:** Territories ruled or sponsored by an Ascended being (a "Patron") who provides protection in exchange for the belief, tribute, and reverence of the populace. Examples include the *Patreonate of Lonely Sandbairsen*, *Patreonate of Solitaires*, and *Asmosia*.
*   **Protectorates:** Isolated or specific realms entirely under the defensive umbrella of a singular, powerful Lord or deity-like figure, such as the *Protectorate of the Realm of Lord Goyoo*.
*   **Dominions:** Areas of absolute control by an entity, often smaller but denser in magical presence, like the *Dominion of Tavern at Waterwheel* or the *Dominion of Lightown*.

## Major Regions

The continent is broadly divided into grand cardinal regions, each hosting unique climates and cultures:
*   **The Southwest:** Characterized by grassy hills bordering vast deserts. Home to the Realm of Lord Goyoo and the Tavern at Waterwheel.
*   **The Southeast & The Great South:** Encompasses vast sand dunes, the Lonely Sandbairsen, and shores touching the Sea of Sorrow.
*   **The Northeast & The Great North:** Frostlands, glaciers, and the domain of the Mistress of Winter.
*   **The Center:** A mountainous, heavily populated region serving as a crossroads.
*   **The Backworld:** A mysterious, shadowy collection of lands hosting the Casino Lights and the Sanguine Swamp.

## Major Cities

*   **Sebenitia (Seat of Culture):** A holy, highly cultured metropolis renowned for its grand temples, the Forge of Sebenitia, and its learned populace.
*   **Lightown:** The "City filled with Lights," a dominion that acts as a beacon of order, faith, and potentially blinding dogma (channeling the Light element).
*   **Crocodopolis:** A dangerous, sprawling settlement within the swamps and rivers, governed heavily by the robust and aggressive locals.
*   **Chacoalia:** The grand capital of the Chacals, located in the deep desert.

## The Distant Reaches (Endgame Zones)

In the extreme, isolated corners of the world, geography becomes terrifyingly bizarre. These zones have been warped by millennial exposure to concentrated belief, power, and energies seeping directly from the elemental planes.
*   **Pink Sky Beaches:** Surreal coastal domains steeped in ancient magic, home to the Milleniums.
*   **Chaotic Abysses:** Nightmarish, fractured rifts in reality where the Golliaggves dwell.
*   **Glass Towers:** Colossal, artificial monuments erected by wildly powerful Ascended creatures (like the Great City of Glass) to serve as their physical domains.

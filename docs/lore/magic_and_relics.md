# Magic and Relics

## Magic System Specifics
Being virtuous is key to a prosperous life. Through emotion, good habits, and virtue, beings can unlock a relationship with the elemental realms, allowing them to tap into elemental power. Virtues have been an object of study for many eras, with different schools theorizing on their nature and mysteries.

Staying in the dimension for prolonged periods loosens one's grip on reality. Being in constant, true contact with the Virtues and tapping power from the elemental realms exacerbates humans' true nature, making homecoming less of a priority over time.

### Majestic Virtues & Elements
*   **WIND (Diligentia):** Ideals of Freedom, Serenity, Independence.
*   **WATER (Moderatio):** Ideals of Moderation, Temperance.
*   **WOOD (Generositas/Benevolentia):** Ideals of Generosity, Abundance, Charity, Plenty.
*   **STEEL (Resolutio):** Ideals of Resolve, Creativity, Innovation, Progress, Revolution.
*   **THUNDER (Justitia):** Ideals of Fairness, Justice, Drive, Impartiality.
*   **Other Elements:** FIRE, EARTH, ICE, LIGHT, DARKNESS.

## Notable Relics of Power

Relics, items, and artifacts known as **Ominoujies** are highly sought after. Masters in special are avid collectors of Ominoujies and hoard great stashes of them to display power and wealth.

*   **Orb of Light:** An ominoujie crafted by the Tower Mage and human allies. It must be dipped in several different springs of water (like the Roadside Fountain and Abandoned Zoo Fountain) to be empowered before being brandished against the Cosmo Queen.
*   **Crowns:** Legendary relics including the *Crown of Cosmos*, *Crown of Etimos*, and *Crown of Sunburn*.
*   **Rings & Gems:** *Ring of Ronn*, *Red Sovereign Gems* (owned by The Hidden King).
*   **Winter Night Dragon Sword:** A powerful sword with Empowering and Swiftness properties, owned by the Mistress of Winter.
*   **Winged Boots:** Precious boots that grant Avoidability and Swiftness.
*   **Terrorlands Cloak of Terror:** Owned by the Mayor Vampire.
*   **Other Relics:** *Trumpet of the Moon*, *Sword (from Molten Bowl)*.

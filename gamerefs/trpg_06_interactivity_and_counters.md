# Aspect 06: Interactivity, Counter-Attacks & Reactions

A comparative guide on reaction abilities, counter-attacks, mid-turn interrupts, body blocking, and co-op strikes across TRPGs.

---

## 1. Enemy-Turn Reaction & Retaliation Systems

Mechanics allowing entities to retaliate, defend, or react when targeted on an enemy turn.

### Approach 1.1: Automatic Range-Based Counter-Attacks
Defenders automatically retaliate during an enemy's attack if the attacker is within the defender's equipped weapon range or through specific counter traits.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Fire Emblem Series, Final Fantasy Tactics, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Breath of Fire Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Langrisser Series, Feda: Emblem of Justice, Chrono Trigger, Digimon World 3, Dragon Quest 9*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Fire Emblem / Vandal Hearts:* Defender counters immediately after receiving a hit if weapon range matches.
  - *FFT:* Requires equipping Reaction Abilities (e.g. *Counter*, *Counter Flood*, *Hamedo*), which proc based on Brave %.
  - *Dragon Quest 9:* Weapons and shield skills dictate block and counter chances automatically during enemy turns.
  - *Chrono Trigger:* Equipping the Rage Band grants a % chance to counter physical attacks.

### Approach 1.2: Priority Reaction Moves & Contact Retaliation
Defenders execute priority reaction moves (*Protect*, *Counter*, *Mirror Coat*, *Sucker Punch*) or retaliate passively through contact abilities (*Rough Skin*, *Static*).
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series (Mainline & Spinoffs)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Mainline:* *Counter* reflects 2x physical damage; *Mirror Coat* reflects 2x special damage; *Protect* negates attacks; *Sucker Punch* strikes first if target is preparing an attack. Contact abilities (*Iron Barbs*, *Static*) damage or paralyze attackers upon contact.

### Approach 1.3: Real-Time Reflect & Auto-Cast Triggers (MMOs)
Taking damage triggers equipped items or skills to automatically reflect damage, cast spells, or consume potions in real-time.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Ragnarok Online, MapleStory*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Ragnarok Online:* Crusaders use *Reflect Shield* to bounce damage back in real-time. Cards like *Mutant Dragonoid* grant a 5% chance to auto-cast Fireball when attacking, while other cards auto-cast defensive skills when taking damage.
  - *MapleStory:* Pets can be programmed to automatically consume HP/MP potions when bars drop below a certain threshold, completely automating real-time survival reactions.

### Not Applicable / Absent
No counter-attacks or mid-turn reaction systems exist; defender takes damage passively without retaliating.
* **Games:** *Into the Breach, Shining Force Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Energy Breaker, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Triangle Strategy, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Dofus & Wakfu, Wild Arms XF, Yggdra Union, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

## 2. Positional Co-op & Intercept Systems

Rules governing joint attacks triggered by ally alignment or grid movement restrictions.

### Approach 2.1: Position-Based Flanking, Team & Session Attacks
Attacking an enemy triggers nearby aligned allies to perform joint follow-up attacks or chained session pushes outside their turn.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Triangle Strategy, Disgaea Series, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Yggdra Union, Chrono Trigger*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Triangle Strategy:* Positioning allies on opposite sides of an enemy triggers automatic **Flanking Follow-up Attacks**.
  - *Disgaea:* Adjacent allies have a % chance to join in a **Team Attack**.
  - *Chrono Trigger:* **Dual & Triple Techs** allow two or three characters to combine their turns and MP to cast massively powerful synchronized attacks, requiring specific party compositions.

### Approach 2.2: Timeline Interruptions & Action Cancellations
Attacking an enemy currently channeling an action on the timeline delays or cancels their upcoming turn.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Grandia Series, Octopath Traveler*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Grandia:* Critical attacks landing between COM and ACT nodes cancel the enemy's queued action.
  - *Octopath Traveler:* Exploiting shield weaknesses triggers a **Break**, skipping the enemy's next turn.

### Approach 2.3: Zone of Control (ZOC) & Physical Grid Pinning
Moving adjacent to an enemy restricts movement or locks units in place.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Wild Arms XF, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Dofus & Wakfu, Ragnarok Online*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Wild Arms XF / Master of Monsters:* Zone of Control (ZOC) prevents moving past adjacent enemy hex tiles.
  - *Dofus & Wakfu:* Moving away from an adjacent enemy checks `Dodge / Lock` ratio; failing pins the character or drains AP/MP.
  - *Ragnarok Online:* Entity collision exists; players cannot walk through mobs, leading to "body blocking" in tight chokepoints during MMO sieges (WoE).

### Not Applicable / Absent
No positional co-op attacks, timeline cancels, or ZOC grid pinning exist.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series, Final Fantasy Tactics, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Into the Breach, Breath of Fire Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Langrisser Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Energy Breaker & Feda: Emblem of Justice, Pokémon Series, Digimon World 3, Dragon Quest 9, MapleStory, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

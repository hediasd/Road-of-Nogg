# Aspect 10: Elemental Systems & Affinity Matrices

A comparative breakdown of elemental wheels, 18-type matrices, terrain interactions, custom spell creation, and deity affinities across TRPGs.

---

## 1. Elemental Advantage Matrices

Architectural systems categorizing elements into advantage/weakness cycles and stat multipliers.

### Approach 1.1: 18-Type Multiplicative Affinity Matrix
18-Type Elemental Matrix where elemental multipliers stack across dual-type defenders (2x, 4x, 0.5x, 0.25x, 0x immunity).
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series (Mainline & Spinoffs)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Series:* 18-Type Matrix (Normal, Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Ice, Fighting, Poison, Ground, Flying, Psychic, Bug, Rock, Ghost, Dragon, Steel, Dark, Fairy). Multipliers stack across dual-type Pokémon (2x, 4x, 0.5x, 0.25x, 0x immunity).

### Approach 1.2: Classic 4/6 Element Advantage Circle
Standard elemental circle (Fire > Wind > Earth > Water > Fire; Light $\leftrightarrow$ Dark) where matching elements deal bonus damage or receive affinity resistance.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Final Fantasy Tactics, Triangle Strategy, Dofus & Wakfu, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth, Wild Arms XF, Energy Breaker, Chrono Trigger, Digimon World 3, Dragon Quest 9, MapleStory*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Tactics Ogre / FFT:* Standard 4/6 element circle.
  - *Hoshigami:* Deity coin alignment alters unit element, changing spell RAP costs and elemental resistances.
  - *Chrono Trigger:* 4-element system (Fire, Water/Ice, Lightning, Shadow). Enemies often absorb their native element and take double damage from the opposite.
  - *Digimon World 3:* 7 elements (Fire, Water, Ice, Wind, Thunder, Machine, Dark) acting as direct resistances. Striking weaknesses is necessary for boss fights.
  - *Dragon Quest 9:* 7 elemental attributes; using a matching elemental weapon or spell (e.g., Frizz against a Fire-weak enemy) applies a fractional damage multiplier.
  - *MapleStory:* Monsters have specific Elemental attributes (Physical, Holy, Fire, Poison, Ice, Lightning, Dark) where attacks can be Strong (1.5x), Normal (1.0x), Weak (0.5x), or Immune (0x).

### Approach 1.3: Dynamic String & Custom Word-Parsing Magic
Spells are constructed dynamically by players writing custom text strings, which the game parses into elements, ranges, and power levels.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Treasure of the Rudras (Rudra no Hidou)*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Treasure of the Rudras:* Kotodama system allows players to type custom 6-letter words; the game engine parses prefixes and suffixes to determine spell element (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth, Light, Dark), MP cost, and AoE shape.

### Approach 1.4: Material Advantage Triangles (Silk > Wood > Iron)
Units and attacks belong to material categories forming a rock-paper-scissors advantage triangle.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Kartia: The Word of Fate*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Kartia:* Material advantage triangle: Silk > Wood > Iron > Silk.

### Approach 1.5: Multi-Level Elemental Property Matrix (MMOs)
Complex grids where elements have varying levels of intensity, drastically shifting damage multipliers.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Ragnarok Online*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Ragnarok Online:* 10 distinct elements (Neutral, Water, Earth, Fire, Wind, Poison, Holy, Shadow, Ghost, Undead), each with 4 escalating levels of intensity (e.g., Fire 1 vs Fire 4). Attack elements multiply against defender element levels, resulting in massive damage scaling (up to 200% damage) or complete immunity (0%).

### Not Applicable / Absent
Combats use non-elemental stat calculations or physical weapon triangles instead of elemental damage wheels.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series (Weapon Triangle instead), Shining Force Series, TearRing Saga & Berwick Saga, Disgaea Series, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Into the Breach, Breath of Fire Series, Langrisser Series, Vandal Hearts Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Yggdra Union, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Feda: Emblem of Justice, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

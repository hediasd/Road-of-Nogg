# Aspect 13: Environment, Weather & Global Field Laws

A comparative breakdown of terrain modifiers, weather systems, surface hazards, destructible cover, and global field laws across TRPGs.

---

## 1. Environmental Tile & Weather Interactions

Rules governing how spells, time cycles, or field conditions alter physical grid tiles or apply global damage multipliers.

### Approach 1.1: Global Weather, Time of Day & Terrain Modifiers
Field weather conditions or time cycles apply global damage multipliers, vision restrictions, or stat boosts to matching elemental types.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Pokémon Series, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together / Reborn, Final Fantasy Tactics, Advance Wars Series, Ogre Battle / Ogre Battle 64*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Pokémon Series:* Field Weather (Harsh Sunlight boosts Fire/weakens Water; Rain boosts Water/weakens Fire). Terrains (Grassy, Electric) modify move power.
  - *Tactics Ogre / FFT:* Dynamic weather (Rain/Snow) alters elemental spell damage and accuracy for specific elements (e.g., Rain boosts Water/Lightning).
  - *Advance Wars Series:* Rain severely restricts Fog of War vision ranges; Snow heavily increases movement costs globally across all terrain types.
  - *Ogre Battle Series:* The Day/Night cycle explicitly alters the strength of Light/Dark aligned units, and triggers specific character transformations (e.g., Fighters turning into Werewolves at night).

### Approach 1.2: Surface Element Modifications (Burning, Freezing, Electrifying)
Casting elemental spells alters physical grid tile properties, creating surface hazards or conductive tile reactions.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Triangle Strategy, Dofus & Wakfu, Ragnarok Online, Bahamut Lagoon, Divinity: Original Sin 1 & 2*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Triangle Strategy:* Fire spells burn grass; Ice spells freeze water into ice; Lightning spells conduct through water pools.
  - *Bahamut Lagoon:* A pioneer in this space. Casting Fire melts ice and evaporates water, Ice freezes water (allowing ground units to walk on it), and Lightning destroys buildings and bridges.
  - *Divinity: Original Sin 1 & 2:* The modern gold standard for surface chemistry. Combining elements creates complex chained reactions (e.g., Poison Gas + Fire = Explosion, Water + Cold = Ice slip hazard, Blood + Lightning = Electrified Blood).
  - *Dofus & Wakfu:* Certain classes place visible traps or elemental glyphs on tiles that trigger effects.
  - *Ragnarok Online:* AoE spells like Fire Pillar, Quagmire (slows MS/ASPD), or Sanctuary act as persistent terrain effects.

### Approach 1.3: Geo Panel & Tile Affinity Buffs
Grid tiles feature color-coded panels or hexes that grant field-wide buffs, hazards (Invincible, ATK +50%, Damage 20%), or elemental affinities.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Disgaea Series, Wild Arms XF, Berwick Saga, Master of Monsters / Nectaris, Yggdra Union*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Disgaea:* Destroying Geo Symbols triggers chain-reaction tile color changes and map damage.
  - *Master of Monsters / Nectaris:* Hexes grant explicit defense % boosts (e.g., Mountains +40% DEF).

### Approach 1.4: Directional / Elevation Terrain Advantage
Terrain explicitly grants damage or accuracy bonuses based on height difference (High Ground) or backstabs.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Final Fantasy Tactics, Tactics Ogre, Vandal Hearts Series, Stella Deus: The Gate of Eternity, Triangle Strategy, Hoshigami: Ruining Blue Earth*

### Approach 1.5: Jump Puzzles and Platforms
Terrain is primarily navigated vertically and horizontally in real-time, relying on character movement physics.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *MapleStory*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *MapleStory:* 2D platforming, flash jumping, and teleporting across platforms. Jump Quests are pure environmental puzzles.

### Approach 1.6: Destructible Cover & Movement Pipes
Grid layout emphasizes using the environment for mobility and line-of-sight blockage, which can be dynamically destroyed.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Mario + Rabbids:* Units slide through movement pipes to bypass map geometry and take cover behind physical blocks, which can be destroyed by weapon fire to expose enemies.

### Approach 1.7: Air/Ground/Space Terrain Types
Units possess specific adaptations for Space, Air, Ground, or Water, affecting their movement capacity and evasion capabilities drastically.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Super Robot Wars Series*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *Super Robot Wars:* Units and pilots have grades (A, B, C, S) for different terrains (Air, Ground, Water, Space). Being in Space vs Ground affects evasion rates and weapon damage output.

### Approach 1.8: Global Field Laws & Artificial Rules
The battlefield enforces artificial "Laws" that restrict specific actions (e.g., "No Fire Magic", "No Swords") independent of physical weather.
* **Games Following This Approach:** *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance (FFTA) / FFTA2*
* **Detailed Variations:**
  - *FFTA:* The Judge system acts as a global field condition. Violating the daily law results in immediate harsh penalties (yellow/red cards, sending units to prison), forcing players to dynamically alter their team composition and tactics based on the active law.

### Not Applicable / Absent
Grid tiles have uniform environmental properties with no element surface hazards, weather systems, or Geo Panels.
* **Games:** *Fire Emblem Series, Shining Force Series, Grandia Series & Octopath Traveler, Into the Breach, Breath of Fire Series, Treasure of the Rudras, Langrisser Series, Knights in the Nightmare, Phantom Brave / Makai Kingdom, Energy Breaker & Feda: Emblem of Justice, Chrono Trigger, Digimon World 3, Dragon Quest 9, Panzer Dragoon Saga, Sakura Wars, Threads of Fate, Vagrant Story, Phantasy Star Series, Road of Nogg (current baseline)*

---

## Implementation Takeaways for Road of Nogg

- Current terrain distinguishes clear, line-of-sight-blocking obstacles, and non-walkable abyss tiles.
- Movement costs, elevation effects, weather, and destructible terrain remain separate future decisions.
